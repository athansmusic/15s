WHAT IS 15 SECOND MYSTERIES??
The most efficient storytelling you'll ever hear.

15 Second Mysteries is a fully produced fiction anthology podcast where each episode is a complete, standalone mystery in exactly fifteen seconds: the crime, the investigation, the twist and the solution. Every episode has a new case, new characters and a whole new world, all built with a full cast, original music and detailed sound design.

WITH EPISODES THIS SHORT, HOW CAN YOU ENSURE THERE'LL BE ENOUGH SUSPENSE FOR EACH CASE??

SEASON ONE
Season One will have 90 episodes, each exactly 15 seconds long (promise). That's about 22 minutes and 30 seconds of total content. The Kickstarter funds the full first season. Most of any extra funding goes toward more episodes. Our goal and commitment is to deliver as much 15 Second Mysteries as possible, fifteen seconds at a time.


HOW DOES $15 GET THE SEASON MADE??
You may be wondering; how does $15 get the season made? Our math says that's about 5 Monster Energy Drinks, pushed potentially to six if you got them at a discount. With these energy drinks, we would be able to maximize our time, and finish the season in a day or two.

WHAT ABOUT THE ADS??
Sometimes you get a little tired of the plot breaking up the commercials; we understand that. So, twice a week, we'll have an episode where you actually have to find the plot within the ads. We think that's funny, and it also helps the people involved fund their creative endeavors to bring about more content.

IS THIS A JOKE??
Honestly? Yes. However, this is a joke that we are taking seriously. We've ran this by some folks, and there seems to be a certain level of enjoyment and community that is able to be built off these 15 second stories. Are we attempting to capitalize on it, so that we have more free time to make more creative works? 100%. The big ask from us would be, please do not back this Kickstarter unless it is absolutely disposable income.

WHY ARE YOU DOING THIS??
At the end of the day, we're making this for us: every time we start to brainstorm a concept, we have a ton of fun, and we figured we'd make it official and just go for it. We'll be able to make this show just fine either way, and we're going to have a lot of these coming out at some point.
